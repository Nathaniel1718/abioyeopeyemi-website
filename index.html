About

Abíóyè Ọpẹ́yẹmí

Linguist at the University of Ibadan working on Yorùbá language technology, phonetics, phonology and discourse analysis.

I transcribe, annotate and analyse speech data with tools such as ELAN and Praat, translate between Yorùbá and English, and build free Yorùbá language tools, including dictionaries and a grapheme-to-phoneme transcriber, for students and researchers.
