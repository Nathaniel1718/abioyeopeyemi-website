Dòdò   Consists of ripe plantains that are fried until they are golden-brown and caramelized.[dòdò] 

n. fried plantain. Ọ̀nà tó dára jùlọ láti jẹ ẹ̀wà àti ìrẹsì ni pẹ̀lú dòdò ti a ti ge sí wẹ́wẹ́ The best way to eat beans and rice is with a side of diced dodo.

Dòdò Ìkirè   [dòdò ìkīɹè] A snack made from overripe plantain.

n. fried plantain of Ikire. a ra dòdò Ìkirè ní ojú ọ̀nà wa we bought dòdò ìkirè on our way.

Dùndún   Dundun is a popular Nigerian snack made from deep-fried white yam. It is crispy outside, soft inside, and usually eaten with spicy pepper sauce (ata dindin).[dũ̀dṹ] 

n. Fried Yam. A ra dùndun àti ata díndín ní ọjà We bought dùndún and sauce from the market.
