Gbẹ̀gìrì   n. [g͡bɛ̀gìɹì] traditional, savory Nigerian soup originating from the Yoruba people, made by boiling, peeling, and blending beans—typically black-eyed or brown peas—into a smooth, thick paste. ọbẹ̀ gbẹ̀gìrì jẹ́ ọbẹ̀ kìki Gbẹ̀gìrì is a thick soup.
