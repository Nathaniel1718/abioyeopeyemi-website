Gbánúnù   [g͡bánṹnũ̀] a traditional medicinal dark soup from Ondo State, Nigeria, renowned as a colon cleanser or detoxifier.

n. White butterfly leaf soup. Kílódé tí o fi fẹ́ràn ọbẹ̀ gbánúnù Why do you love white butterfly soup so much?
