Ògì   [ògì] A traditional fermented cereal pudding or porridge, widely known as pap or akamu in other parts of Nigeria.

n. Pap. Ní gbogbo òwúrọ̀ ọjọ́ ẹtì, òórùn ògì gbígbóná àti àkàrà didin máà n kún ilé wa Every Saturday morning, the aroma of hot ògì and fried àkàrà fills our home.

n. starchy cassava meal. màá jẹ fùfú ní ọ̀la I will east fùfú tomorrow.

Ọ̀rúnlá   [ɔ̀ɹṹlá] a traditional delicacy made from dehydrated ground okra, which can be flavoured with palm oil, assorted meat, fish and cow skin.

n. Dehydrated okra soup. Wọ́n n ta ọbẹ̀ ọ̀rúnlá. They sell Dehydrated Okra soup.

n. yam fritters.[ɔ̀dʒɔ̀dʒɔ̀]  ọ̀jọ̀jọ̀ jẹ́ oúnjẹ yorùbá pọ́nbélé ọ̀jọ̀jọ̀ is a typical yoruba food.

ọ̀wọ̀wọ̀   [ɔ̀wɔ̀wɔ̀] A traditional snack made from boiled, dried corn (or fresh sweet corn) mixed with grated coconut.

n. Corn and coconut. Ẹ jẹ́ ká fí ọ̀wọ̀wọ̀ diẹ ṣe ipanu ọ̀sán Let's have some ọ̀wọ̀wọ̀ for an afternoon snack.
