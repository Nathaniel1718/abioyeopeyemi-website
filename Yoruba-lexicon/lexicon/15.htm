Ògì   n. [ògì] A traditional fermented cereal pudding or porridge, widely known as pap or akamu in other parts of Nigeria. Ní gbogbo òwúrọ̀ ọjọ́ ẹtì, òórùn ògì gbígbóná àti àkàrà didin máà n kún ilé wa Every Saturday morning, the aroma of hot ògì and fried àkàrà fills our home.

Ọbẹ̀ Ìgbá   n. [ɔ̄bɛ̀ ìg͡bá] A starchy food made with cassava. màá jẹ fùfú ní ọ̀la I will east fùfú tomorrow.

Ọ̀rúnlá   n. [ɔ̀ɹṹlá] a traditional delicacy made from dehydrated ground okra, which can be flavoured with palm oil, assorted meat, fish and cow skin. Wọ́n n ta ọbẹ̀ ọ̀rúnlá. They sell Dehydrated Okra soup.

ọ̀jọ̀jọ̀   n. A popular Yoruba snack or finger food made from grated water yam.[ɔ̀dʒɔ̀dʒɔ̀]  ọ̀jọ̀jọ̀ jẹ́ oúnjẹ yorùbá pọ́nbélé ọ̀jọ̀jọ̀ is a typical yoruba food.

ọ̀wọ̀wọ̀   n. [ɔ̀wɔ̀wɔ̀] A traditional snack made from boiled, dried corn (or fresh sweet corn) mixed with grated coconut. Ẹ jẹ́ ká fí ọ̀wọ̀wọ̀ diẹ ṣe ipanu ọ̀sán Let's have some ọ̀wọ̀wọ̀ for an afternoon snack.
