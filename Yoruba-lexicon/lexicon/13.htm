Marúgbó   n. A Yoruba soup made from black seed leaves.[máɹúg͡bó]  mo ní márúgbó sílé I have black leaves soup at home.

Mọ́ínmọ́ín   n. [mɔ́ĩ́mɔ́ĩ́] Moin moin is a popular Nigerian dish made from steamed blended beans. It is a nutritious, protein-rich pudding from southwestern Nigeria. Mọ́ínmọ́ín jẹ́ èròjà óúnjẹ tó dáa tí a fi ẹ̀wà ṣe. Mọ́ínmọ́ín is a healthy food made from beans.
