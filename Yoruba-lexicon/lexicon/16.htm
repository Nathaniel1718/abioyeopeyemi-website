Púpurú   n. [k͡púk͡pūɹú] Pupuru is a traditional cassava flour food from Ondo State. It is fermented and smoked, giving it a light texture and smoky taste, and is eaten as a 'swallow' with soups. Púpurú tí mo rà láti Ìpínlẹ̀ Ondo dùn gidi gan. The púpurú I bought from Ondo state is very delicious.
