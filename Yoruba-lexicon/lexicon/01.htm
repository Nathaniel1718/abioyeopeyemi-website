Àsáró   n. Asaro is a traditional Yoruba dish made from white yam cooked and mashed in a rich sauce of palm oil, tomatoes, peppers, and onions. It is a thick, savory yam porridge cooked as a one-pot meal.[àsáɹó]  Àsárò jẹ́ oúnjẹ àtàárọ̀ pàtàkì lọ́jọ́rú ní ilé wa Asaro is a Wednesday morning special in my home.
