Ata ẹ̀wà   n. [ātā ɛ̀wà] A yoruba stew specifically made for eating beans. Mo fẹ́ran ata ẹ̀wà I love beans stew.

Ayamaṣe   n. [ājāmāʃē] A yoruba stew made for ofada rice. Mi ò fẹ́ran Ayamaṣe I don't like ofada stew.

Àádùn   n. [ǎːdũ̀] A crunchy, savory, and slightly sweet treat often shaped into small balls or patties. It's name implies 'sweetness'. mo ra àádùn ní ọjà I bought sweets from the martket.

Àbàrí   n. [àbàɹí] A food made with blended maize. mo ra Àbàrí jẹ. I bought Àbàrí to eat.

Àbùlà   n. [àbùlà] combination of Beans soup and jute(Ewedu). wọ́n ń ta àbùlà ni ibí they sell àbùlà here.

Àdàlú   n. [àdàlú] A popular Yoruba one-pot porridge dish featuring a mixture of beans and corn. Àdàlú wùn mí jẹ I crave àdàlú.

Àpọ̀n   n. [àk͡pɔ̃̀] Apon soup (Ogbono soup) is a popular Yoruba “draw” soup made from ground African mango seeds. It is thick, nutritious, and known for its slimy texture, similar to okra soup. Mi ò fẹ́ràn ọbẹ̀ Àpọ̀n gan-an. I don't really like Àpọ̀nSoup.

Àsáró   n. [àsáɹó] Asaro is a traditional Yoruba dish made from white yam cooked and mashed in a rich sauce of palm oil, tomatoes, peppers, and onions. It is a thick, savory yam porridge cooked as a one-pot meal. Àsárò jẹ́ oúnjẹ àtàárọ̀ pàtàkì lọ́jọ́rú ní ilé wa Asaro is a Wednesday morning special in my home.

Àsun   n. [àsũ̄] A meal prepare from roasted and pepped goat meat. kílódé tí o fi jẹ àsun? Why did you eat roasted goat meat?

Àmàlà dúdú   n. [àmàlà dúdú] A staple food made with yam peel. Mo jẹ àmàlà dúdú ni àná i ate black Àmàlà yesterday.
