Àpọ̀n

n. [àk͡pɔ̃̀]

Apon soup (Ogbono soup) is a popular Yoruba “draw” soup made from ground African mango seeds. It is thick, nutritious, and known for its slimy texture, similar to okra soup.

Ẹ̀kọ
