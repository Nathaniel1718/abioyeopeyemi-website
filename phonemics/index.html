Data in phonemic transcription

Entering data

  • One entry per line; the gloss can be in quotes or after a tab or dash.
  • Brackets [ ] or slashes / / around the form are optional.
  • Diacritics stay with their sound: pʰ, aː, ã, k͡p.
  • List sequences such as ts or kp under “Treat as one segment” if they are single sounds.
  • Lines starting with # are ignored. Your data is saved in this browser.

How verdicts are reached

  1. Minimal pairs with different meanings: separate phonemes.
  2. Identical environments (same sound on both sides): contrast is likely.
  3. Same meaning, either sound: free variation.
  4. Never in the same environment, with a clear conditioning side: complementary distribution, so allophones.
  5. Otherwise the result is inconclusive and more data is needed.

Good practice

  • The tool drafts rules; check that each one makes phonetic sense.
  • The allophone with the wider distribution is suggested as the basic form; you can choose the other.
  • Small data sets give tentative results: phones that occur only once or twice are flagged.
